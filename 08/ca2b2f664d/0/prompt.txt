### Compact transcript written without secret redaction at condensation time

**High Severity**

The compact transcript at condensation time is generated from the raw, unredacted `sessionData.Transcript` via `compactTranscriptForV2`, and `compact.Compact` performs no secret redaction. The resulting bytes are written directly to git via `CreateBlobFromContent` in `writeMainSessionToSubdirectory` without any call to `redact.JSONLBytes`. In contrast, the raw transcript is always redacted before being persisted (v1 in `writeTranscript`, v2 in `writeTranscriptBlobs`), and the stop-time finalization path correctly redacts the transcript _before_ compaction. This means secrets present in the transcript could be persisted unredacted to the v2 `/main` ref during condensation.
